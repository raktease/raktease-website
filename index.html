HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Important details to know before requesting a blood test.

Is my appointment confirmed after submitting?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your request. Your appointment is confirmed only after the team checks availability and replies.

How do I know if I need fasting?

Fasting depends on the specific test and your clinician's instructions. Ask the team before your collection; do not fast unnecessarily.

Can I type a test not listed here?

Yes. Type the test name in the booking field. The team will confirm whether it is available and share the price.

Are the package prices fixed?

Prices are not displayed in this demo. Confirm current pricing, test inclusions, collection charges and report timelines before booking.
